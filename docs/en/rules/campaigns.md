# Campaign scenarios

Strategic Surprise, Extended Buildup, and War of Nerves each have 1983 and 1988 versions. The selected year determines the unit roster, printed strengths, and reinforcement schedule.

All six options start on war turn one and last fourteen war turns. The first Joint Reinforcement Phase places the opening forces; later units arrive on their scheduled turns by ground, rail, air, or sea (see [Joint Reinforcements](reinforcements.md)). Reforger units stand at their Reforger sites. The map, movement, and combat sequence use the usual rules.

| Scenario | WP Airlift Commands | NATO Airlift Commands |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Strategic Surprise | 3 | 2 |
| Extended Buildup | 4 | 3 |
| War of Nerves | 3 | 2 |

In Strategic Surprise, WP attacks receive the Surprise modifier on the first war turn.

WP Front HQs and U.S. V/VII Corps HQs can provide Offensive Support to subordinate units. Baltic Front and U.S. III Corps HQs provide support only in Extended Buildup; the Baltic Corps HQ never provides support.

## Fortified hexes (Extended Buildup)

In Extended Buildup, NATO has fortified the inner-German border. Every West German hex next to the Iron Curtain, plus West Berlin, starts the game Fortified. The map marks each Fortified hex with a steel-grey inner border. Fortified hexes help only NATO units defending against the Warsaw Pact:

- Warsaw Pact air and artillery strikes on NATO units in a Fortified hex get −2 on the die, in place of the terrain modifier. A unit under a train marker still gets +1 instead.
- A Warsaw Pact attack on a Fortified hex shifts two columns down, in place of the terrain shift.
- NATO Soft units defending in a Fortified hex double their Defense Strength.
- Retreats from a Fortified hex are reduced by one hex, or by two if the hex is Mountain or a Major or Key City. A retreating unit may also stop in a Fortified hex after one hex.
- Fortification does not change movement costs.

In each Joint Status Phase, every Fortified hex that holds a Warsaw Pact unit is Conquered and stops being Fortified for the rest of the game, even if NATO retakes it. Because the check comes only at the start of a turn, NATO always gets one turn to recapture a lost hex before it loses its Fortified status.

West Berlin is a Major City, so being Fortified gives it no extra benefit.
