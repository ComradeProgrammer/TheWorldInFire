# Joint Reinforcements

The Joint Reinforcement Phase follows the Joint Status Phase in every game turn. It requires no player decision. Every unit scheduled to arrive in the current turn automatically enters play in its assigned map hex.

Opening deployment uses the same reinforcement procedure. The first Joint Reinforcement Phase automatically places the opening units defined by the selected scenario. A unit may enter either an assigned map hex or its side's Strategic Reserve.

In BALTAP 1983, turn one places seventeen units on the map and ten in the Strategic Reserve. Later reinforcements enter the Strategic Reserve. The rules prototype scenario places two opening units:

- the Soviet 6th Guards Motor Rifle Division in hex 2806;
- the West German 1st Brigade, 1st Panzer Division in hex 3216.

A unit enters play using its first strength step. A unit state can record one or more strength steps, each listing attack, defense, and movement values in that order.
