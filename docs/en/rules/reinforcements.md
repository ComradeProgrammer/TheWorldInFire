# Joint Reinforcements

The Joint Reinforcement Phase follows the Joint Status Phase in every game turn. It requires no player decision. Every unit scheduled to arrive in the current turn automatically enters play in its assigned map hex.

Opening deployment uses the same reinforcement procedure. The first Joint Reinforcement Phase automatically places the opening units defined by the selected scenario. A unit may enter either an assigned map hex or its side's Strategic Reserve.

Later reinforcements arrive in one of these ways:

- **Ground** (including reserve sites and Reforger sites): the unit appears in its designated hex. A unit arriving through a Reinforcement Sector appears at that sector's entry hex on the map edge, marked R1 to R5 on the map (NATO sectors 1 and 2 in the west, Warsaw Pact sectors 3 to 5 in the east).
- **Rail**: the unit appears at its sector's entry hex already Entrained, and may move by rail in its side's next Battle Planning Phase. If its side's rail capacity is full, it arrives Entraining instead.
- **Air and Sea**: the unit enters the Strategic Reserve and comes onto the map by Air Transport, Paradrop, or Sea Transport (see [Movement](movement.md)).

If the designated hex is full, the unit takes the nearest hex with room, up to three hexes away. If the hex holds an enemy unit or is an enemy-controlled city, or no hex nearby has room, the unit enters the Strategic Reserve instead. The opening deployment is placed exactly as the scenario sets it up.

In BALTAP 1983, turn one places seventeen units on the map and ten in the Strategic Reserve. Later reinforcements enter the Strategic Reserve. The rules prototype scenario places two opening units:

- the Soviet 6th Guards Motor Rifle Division in hex 2806;
- the West German 1st Brigade, 1st Panzer Division in hex 3216.

A unit enters play using its first strength step. A unit state can record one or more strength steps, each listing attack, defense, and movement values in that order.
