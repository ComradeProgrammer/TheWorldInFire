# Artillery

Artillery divisions are combat units that fight only by firing **Artillery Strikes**. They never make ground attacks, but they defend like any other combat unit when the enemy attacks their hex.

## Firing

In its own Offensive Strike Phase, the acting side may fire each of its artillery divisions once, in any order, before ending the phase. An artillery division fires on its own: it does not need any other friendly unit in its hex.

To fire, choose:

- a **target hex** adjacent to the artillery division that contains enemy units. Artillery cannot fire across a Blocked or All-Sea hexside, nor across the Danish Ferry. Rivers and other hexsides do not weaken it;
- up to **two enemy steps** in that hex. The first unit named takes any step loss. An HQ must be targeted alone.

An artillery division cannot fire if it:

- has already fired this game turn;
- is Suppressed;
- is Out of Combat Supply;
- is under a train marker;
- is not on the map.

## Resolution

An Artillery Strike is resolved exactly like a fighter-bomber's ground strike (see [Air Power](air-power.md)): roll a d6, apply the same target modifiers (terrain or train marker, the striking side's Airspace over the target hex, and the Surprise-turn bonus), and read the same table:

| Modified roll | Result |
| --- | --- |
| 1 or less | No effect |
| 2–4 | Disrupt every target |
| 5 or more | First target loses one step; disrupt the remaining targets |

If the strike removes the last enemy unit from the hex, place a Breakthrough Marker there.

## Suppression

When an artillery division would be Disrupted, by an enemy strike or by a battle, it is **Suppressed** instead. A Suppressed artillery division cannot fire, defends at half strength, and may move only one hex by Minimum movement. Suppressed markers are removed in their side's Post-Battle Phase.

## Defending

Artillery divisions never attack, never Counterattack, and are never committed to a battle. When their hex is attacked they add their full Defense Strength, exactly like a Maneuver unit, whether or not other units share the hex. The usual halving for Disruption, Suppression, and Out of Combat Supply applies. They take step losses in the same order as Maneuver units, strongest first.
