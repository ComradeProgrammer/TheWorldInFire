# BALTAP 1983

BALTAP 1983 is a seven-turn introductory scenario focused on Denmark and the Baltic approaches.

The first Joint Reinforcement Phase performs the opening deployment automatically: seventeen units are placed in map hexes and ten units enter their respective side's Strategic Reserve.

Later units also enter the Strategic Reserve automatically during the Joint Reinforcement Phase:

| Turn | New units |
| --- | ---: |
| 2 | 11 |
| 3 | 2 |
| 4 | 2 |
| 5 | 1 |
| 6 | 1 |
| 7 | 0 |

The game ends after all seven turns are completed.
