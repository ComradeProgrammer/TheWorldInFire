# Cities

There are three kinds of city: Minor, Major, and Key. Every city hex is always controlled by one side.

## Free and Conquered Cities

At the start of play, each city is controlled by the side it belongs to and is a **Free City**. Cities in East Germany, Poland, and Czechoslovakia belong to the Warsaw Pact. All other cities, including West Berlin, belong to NATO.

A city held by the other side is a **Conquered City**. When the original owner takes it back, it becomes a Free City again.

## Movement and cities

- No unit may enter an enemy Free City by any kind of movement, even if no enemy unit is there.
- A unit may enter an enemy Conquered City only by Tactical movement. It takes control of every enemy Conquered City it enters, even if it does not stop there. If its side originally owned the city, the city is liberated and becomes a Free City again.
- March and Rail movement may not enter an enemy-controlled city.
- Air transport may not end in an enemy-controlled city.
- A Free City exerts a Zone of Control in its own hex only.
- A friendly Free City lets a Soft unit enter its hex directly from another EZOC hex, just as a friendly unit in the hex would.

Undoing a move also returns control of any city that move took.

## Attack objectives

An enemy Free City may be marked as an attack objective even if no enemy unit occupies it.
