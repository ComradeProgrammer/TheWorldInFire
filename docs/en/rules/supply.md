# Supply

This game uses a simplified supply rule. Only the two alliances matter: units of every nation trace supply the same way, and cities never need supply themselves.

## When supply is checked

Supply is checked for the acting side in its automatic Pre-Battle Phase, at the start of its player turn. The result sets both Movement Supply and Combat Supply and lasts until that side's next Pre-Battle Phase. Units in the Strategic Reserve are always supplied, and reinforcements arrive supplied.

An out-of-supply unit carries a red **OOS** tab on its counter.

## Line of Supply

A Line of Supply is a path of hexes from the unit to a supply source. It may not enter:

- a hex containing an enemy unit;
- an enemy-controlled city;
- an All-Sea hex, or cross a Blocked or All-Sea hexside (a Causeway may be crossed; only NATO may trace across the Danish Ferry);
- a hex in an enemy zone of control, unless that hex contains a friendly unit or a friendly Free City.

## Supply sources

**A combat unit is in supply** if it can trace a Line of Supply to either:

- a **Free City of its own alliance** within **10 hexes**; or
- a **supplied friendly HQ** within that HQ's **Support Range** (the Attack value printed on its full-strength side). Any friendly HQ can supply any friendly unit. An HQ under a train marker supplies no one, but a Suppressed HQ still supplies.

**An HQ is in supply** if it can trace a Line of Supply of up to **10 hexes** to any city its alliance controls, whether Free or Conquered.

**West Berlin** is an enclave: it supplies only units in its own hex or adjacent to it, and it is never a supply source for an HQ.

Cutting the Line of Supply (surrounding a unit with units and zones of control, or pushing it far from its cities and HQs) puts it out of supply. Destroying an HQ only affects units that relied on it: units still within 10 hexes of a friendly Free City stay supplied.

## Effects

- **Out of Movement Supply:** Tactical movement at half the printed allowance, rounded down; no March movement, entraining, or air transport; cannot be placed in reserve.
- **Out of Combat Supply:** attacks and defends at half strength.
- **Unsupplied HQ:** moves at half its allowance, cannot entrain, cannot give Offensive Support, and supplies no units.
- Only supplied units project Airspace.

A Resupply operation during Battle Planning restores supply to the units in the selected stack until the next check.
