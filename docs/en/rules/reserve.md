# Reserves

A player may hold units back from the fighting so they can move again after combat, to exploit a breakthrough or to close a gap. NATO calls these units **Reserve**; the Warsaw Pact calls them **OMG** (Operational Maneuver Group). The rules are the same for both.

## Placing a unit in reserve

During the Battle Planning Phase, mark a unit as Reserve (NATO) or OMG (Warsaw Pact). A marked unit carries a gold RES or OMG tab on its counter.

A unit may be marked only if all of the following are true:

- it is a Maneuver unit on the map (HQs never go into reserve);
- it has moved **no more than half** of its Movement Allowance this phase, rounded down (a unit that has not moved qualifies). For March movement this is half of the doubled March allowance. A move by Minimum movement uses the whole allowance;
- it has not moved by rail or air transport this phase, and it is not under a train marker;
- it is not Disrupted and not Out of Supply;
- it is **not in an enemy zone of control**. It may start the phase in one, but it must not end its movement there.

If a marked unit later stops qualifying (for example, it moves further than half its allowance or is ordered to entrain), the marker is removed automatically. The marker can also be removed at any time before the Battle Planning Phase ends.

**A unit in reserve cannot attack** in the following Combat Phase.

## Reserve Phase

After the Combat Phase, the acting side moves its marked units:

- Each marked unit may move up to **half its printed Movement Allowance, rounded up**.
- Only **Tactical movement** is allowed, or Minimum movement (one hex, when the unit has not yet moved this phase). March, rail, and air transport are not.
- A unit may move in several orders, and each move can be undone until the phase ends.
- Units move in any direction the player chooses; all normal movement costs, zones of control, and the stacking limit apply.
- **Breakthrough Zone:** a Breakthrough Marker's hex and its six adjacent hexes form a Breakthrough Zone (shaded yellow on the map). **Hard** units do not pay the extra Movement Point for entering or leaving an enemy zone of control in a hex of a Breakthrough Zone. This is how armour exploits a successful attack.

Units without a marker cannot move in the Reserve Phase.

At the end of the Reserve Phase, all of the acting side's Reserve/OMG markers and Breakthrough Markers are removed, together with the enemy's Air Interdiction Zones.

## Post-Battle Phase

The Post-Battle Phase follows automatically. It removes the Suppressed markers from the acting side's HQs, and the player turn ends.
